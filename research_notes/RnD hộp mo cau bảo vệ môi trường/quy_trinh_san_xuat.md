## 1. Process steps and parameters (collection to packing)

### Takeaway
The process is simple and chemical-free: collect fallen sheaths, sort, wash or scrub, soak to soften, partially dry to a mouldable moisture level, hot-press in heated metal dies, trim, sterilise (UV in the Vietnamese plant), dry fully and pack. The one published optimisation found is 170 °C for 2 minutes with sheath moisture of 20–60 wt%. Pressure is not well documented. Vietnamese supplier ads say 2–10 tonnes.

### Cited Findings
**Collection and seasonality**
- Mega Eco (Quảng Ngãi) buys sheaths from local households. In Quảng Ngãi the sheaths fall from March to October. The plant reportedly ran at only about one third of capacity because of raw-material shortage. — [Công Thương](https://congthuong.vn/quang-ngai-bien-mo-cau-thanh-san-pham-xuat-khau-455559.html); [Vietnamnet](https://vietnamnet.vn/bien-mo-cau-thanh-chen-dia-hop-com-xuat-khau-phuc-vu-khach-vip-tren-may-bay-2355944.html); [CafeF](https://cafef.vn/doc-dao-chen-dia-lam-bang-mo-cau-xuat-khau-khap-5-chau-188240513083403552.chn)
- The owner says the biggest difficulty is the shortage of dry sheaths. — [CafeF, Nov 2024](https://cafef.vn/muc-so-thi-quy-trinh-bien-mo-cau-thanh-hop-xop-dung-do-an-188241122150913116.chn)
- Product is a single piece pressed from one naturally fallen *Areca catechu* sheath, with no glue, binder, dye or coating. — [Ecodyne Tableware](https://ecodynetableware.com/areca-leaf-plates/)

**Washing and soaking**
- Vietnamese process (Mega Eco): scrub or wash the sheaths, soak them in water until soft, let them drain, then hot-press them in moulds. — [Công Thương](https://congthuong.vn/quang-ngai-bien-mo-cau-thanh-san-pham-xuat-khau-455559.html); [PLO](https://plo.vn/doc-dao-bien-mo-cau-thanh-chen-dia-xuat-khau-post819844.html)
- One Indian source says sheaths are soaked in water for 2 days to soften, then sun-dried. Other sources give no soaking time. — [EcoCutlery / KKN Exports (search extract)](https://ecocutlery.co.in/manufacturing-process-of-areca-leaf-plates/)

**Pre-press drying and moisture**
- A Thai journal study (TSU) found that sheath moisture before moulding should be 20–60 wt%. — [TSU journal, "Influence of Molding Condition and Moisture Content of Arecanut Leaf Sheath on Quality of Food Plates"](https://ph02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/tsujournal/article/view/254700)
- KKN Exports says residual moisture can harm the plates, and that drying inhibits mould and microbes on the surface. — [KKN Exports blog](https://kknexports.com/blog/manufacturing-process-areca-leaf-plates/)

**Hot-press moulding (temperature, time, pressure)**
- TSU study: tested 150, 160 and 170 °C with 1, 2 and 3 min holds. The best result was **170 °C for 2 min**. — [TSU journal](https://ph02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/tsujournal/article/view/254700)
- D'source (IIT Bombay design gallery): pressing temperature 170–180 °C. — [D'source](https://www.dsource.in/gallery/areca-leaf-plates)
- Ecodyne: pressed at about 200 °C. — [Ecodyne](https://ecodynetableware.com/areca-leaf-plates/)
- Mega Eco (Vietnam): moulds at 80–120 °C, about 40 seconds per press. This is much lower than the other sources and may be a reporting error. — [khoinghiep.net.vn](https://khoinghiep.net.vn/kiem-hang-tram-trieu-dong-tu-mo-cau-tuong-bo-di-a1665.html)
- A Vietnamese machine seller (Quảng Ngãi) advertises 1.5–3 kW, 150–250 °C, **2–10 tonnes** of press force and 300–1,000 pcs/day depending on the machine. These are advertised, unverified figures. — [Tự động hóa Quảng Ngãi](https://tudonghoaquangngai.com.vn/may-ep-mo-cau-tai-quang-ngai/)

**Post-press steps: trimming, sterilisation, storage, packing**
- Mega Eco sterilises finished products with a UV lamp, then packs them. Samples are sent for testing as required before sale. — [Công Thương](https://congthuong.vn/quang-ngai-bien-mo-cau-thanh-san-pham-xuat-khau-455559.html)
- After moulding, workers blow dust off the products. — [CafeF, Nov 2024](https://cafef.vn/muc-so-thi-quy-trinh-bien-mo-cau-thanh-hop-xop-dung-do-an-188241122150913116.chn)
- Store plates only once they are fully dry, to avoid mould and mildew. — [Arbhu Enterprises guide](https://arbhuenterprises.com/blog/a-complete-guide-to-areca-plates-production-usage-and-benefits/)

### Inferences
- A workable starting window is 165–180 °C for 1.5–2 min, with sheath moisture brought into the 20–60% range after soaking. Fix the parameters through your own trials for each die shape and thickness.
- Mould is the main quality risk, especially in Vietnam's humid climate. Controls should cover the pre-press moisture window, full drying after pressing, UV treatment and dry storage. Hot pressing itself also sterilises the surface.

### Gaps
- No primary source gave a validated press pressure (MPa or tonnes per die) or a cooling or demould procedure. The TSU full text may contain these but could not be fetched.
- No standard soaking time was found. The "2 days" figure is a single source, and many producers likely soak for much less.
- No sourced target for storage relative humidity or final moisture content, and no sourced anti-mould treatment other than drying and UV.
- No sourced trimming method (die-cut edge versus separate cutter).

### Inferences
- Capacity claims are inconsistent: 2,000 pcs/h for a ₹1.75 lakh machine is implausible next to 300 pcs/h for a 6-die machine and 200 pcs/day per machine at Mega Eco. For planning, assume about 30–60 cycles/h per die station (1–2 min hold) × number of dies, then derate for loading.
- For a Vietnamese SME, importing 4–10 Indian 2–6-die presses (US$1,000–4,000 each, plus freight and duties) is the proven route (Mega Eco). Chinese fully automatic lines cost 3–5× more per machine.
- Custom food-box shapes (hinged lids, compartments) probably need a custom die quote. The listed ₹2,500 compartment die suggests custom dies are cheap compared with the machines.

### Gaps
- No price list found from Vietnamese machine builders, and no quote for a hinged clamshell (hộp) die.
- No Chinese manufacturer listing with FOB price and model specs was found, only the Alibaba guide ranges.
- Indian listing prices exclude GST, freight and dies unless stated.

## 3. Yield, raw-material cost, labour, energy and unit cost

### Takeaway
A sheath generally yields one large plate (for example a 10″, 25 cm plate). Small items can be cut from offcuts, but no source quantified this. Wastage allowances in studies are 5–8.75%. Sheath cost is ₹1.2–3.2 in India (360–960 VND) and about 1,000 VND in Vietnam. Indian studies put full production cost at ₹0.51–2.85 per plate (≈150–850 VND). Mega Eco sells at 2,000–3,000 VND (US$0.08–0.11) ex-factory.

### Cited Findings
- One single-piece plate per sheath (Ecodyne). Its "10-inch" plate is 25 cm across. — [Ecodyne](https://ecodynetableware.com/areca-leaf-plates/)
- India, sheath price: ₹1.2 per sheath in an economic study (Manjunatha et al.), with 8.75% wastage, raw material at ₹0.000611/cm² and electricity at ₹0.000259 per cm² pressed. — [ResearchGate, Manjunatha G.R. et al.](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/337160284_Eco-friendly_Arecanut_Leaf_Sheath_Products_An_Economic_Analysis)
- India, current price: manufacturers pay ₹2–3.20 per sheath, up from 20 paise 18 years earlier. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india/karnataka/from-tree-to-table-2722601)
- IndiaMART asking prices for dried sheaths: about ₹1.70–4 per piece. — [IndiaMART](https://dir.indiamart.com/impcat/areca-leaves.html)
- ISAE paper (Patil): cost per unit ranged from ₹2.85 (11″) down to ₹0.89 (8×4″). Net return was ₹0.51 per 12″ plate and ₹0.40 per 10″ plate. Total operating cost was ₹21.47 lakh per unit per year, 95.13% of it variable. Wastage was 5%. — [ISAE India](https://isaeindia.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/14-Kiran-Kumar-R-Patil.pdf)
- NCDC sample DPR: starting cost of ₹1.4 per plate, assuming free sheaths from the cooperative's own members. — [NCDC DPR](https://ncdc.in/documents/downloads/12150805206.Sample-DPR_Manufacturing-of-Palm-Leaf-Plate.pdf)
- A Karnataka value-chain study cites 1,825 man-days of employment per year per unit. — [Indian Journals, JRE 61(2)](https://indianjournals.com/article/jre-61-2-014)
- Vietnam: sheath bought at about 1,000 VND each (≈US$0.038). Products sell at 2,000–3,000 VND each. Revenue is more than 2 billion VND per year. — [Công Thương](https://congthuong.vn/quang-ngai-bien-mo-cau-thanh-san-pham-xuat-khau-455559.html); [CafeF](https://cafef.vn/muc-so-thi-quy-trinh-bien-mo-cau-thanh-hop-xop-dung-do-an-188241122150913116.chn)
- Vietnam unit prices range from 1,000 to 6,000 VND depending on the product. — [khoinghiep.net.vn](https://khoinghiep.net.vn/kiem-hang-tram-trieu-dong-tu-mo-cau-tuong-bo-di-a1665.html)
- Vietnam retail: a set of 10 rice trays is about 39,000 VND. A 3-compartment plate is 51,000–485,000 VND depending on pack size. — [Lazada](https://www.lazada.vn/products/hcmkhay-dung-com-bang-mo-cau-set-10-cai-i583434998.html); [ArecaViet](https://arecaviet.com/dia-mo-cau-3-ngan-d30xr25/)
- Vietnam industrial electricity: normal-hour production tariff at 22 kV to under 110 kV is 1,833 VND/kWh (≈US$0.07). The production tariff range is 1,146–3,640 VND/kWh excl. VAT (Decision 1279/QĐ-BCT, 9 May 2025). VnExpress reports a 2% tariff cut from 1 November. — [Tiền Phong](https://tienphong.vn/re-hon-nhieu-gia-dien-kinh-doanh-khach-hang-nao-duoc-ap-gia-dien-san-xuat-post1842772.tpo); [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/gia-dien-giam-2-tu-ngay-mai-5139154.html)

### Inferences (indicative Vietnam unit-cost model, per large plate; not sourced as a whole)
- Raw material: about 1,000 VND per sheath ÷ (1 − 8% waste) ≈ 1,090 VND per plate.
- Energy: a 3–5 kW press doing about 200–300 pcs per 8 h uses roughly 0.1–0.16 kWh per piece. At about 1,833–2,100 VND/kWh that is about 200–330 VND per piece, before the energy used for drying.
- Labour: Mega Eco employs 15–25 workers for 500,000–600,000 pcs/month, about 20,000–40,000 pcs per worker per month. At an **assumed** 7–9 million VND/month per worker (not sourced), labour is about 175–450 VND per piece.
- Total direct cost is therefore about 1,500–2,000 VND per plate before packaging, depreciation and overhead. That fits Mega Eco's 2,000–3,000 VND ex-factory price, so margins are thin and raw material is about 55–70% of direct cost.

### Gaps
- No source quantified pieces per sheath for small items (bowls, spoons, small boxes) or the use of offcuts.
- No Vietnamese wage, labour-per-shift or energy-per-piece data from a producer.
- No cost data for packaging (shrink film, cartons) or for UV equipment.

## 4. Example business plans / project reports (CAPEX, break-even)

### Takeaway
The KVIC/PMEGP model unit costs ₹8.7 lakh (≈US$9,900; ≈261M VND), with 1–2 hydraulic presses and a 59% break-even. Consultant reports range from ₹3.75 lakh for a minimal unit to ₹79 lakh for a larger plant. A Vietnamese SME with 5–10 imported presses plausibly needs about 0.5–2 billion VND, but no Vietnamese plan was found.

### Cited Findings
- **KVIC PMEGP profile:** total ₹8.70 lakh, made up of plant & machinery ₹4.95 lakh, furniture & fixtures ₹0.50 lakh and working capital ₹3.25 lakh. Break-even is 59%. Implementation takes 5–6 months. The machinery line shows 1 hydraulic press at ₹3.45 lakh; the ₹4.95 lakh P&M total is inconsistent with this (perhaps it includes dies and accessories). — [KVIC PMEGP profile](https://www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegp/pmegpweb/docs/commonprojectprofile/ArecaLeafPlates.pdf); [Zee News](https://zeenews.india.com/companies/eco-friendly-business-idea-invest-nearly-rs-90k-earn-upto-rs-5-lakh-in-areca-leaf-plates-manufacturing-business-2664309.html)

**Conversions (approx.):** ₹3.75 lakh ≈ US$4,300 ≈ 113M VND. ₹8.7 lakh ≈ US$9,900 ≈ 261M VND. ₹12 lakh ≈ US$13,600 ≈ 360M VND. ₹18 lakh ≈ US$20,500 ≈ 540M VND. ₹79 lakh ≈ US$90,000 ≈ 2.37 billion VND.

### Inferences
- Break-even estimates range from about 37% to 59% of capacity, depending on wage, sheath price and selling-price assumptions. Raw-material supply, not machinery, is the binding constraint (Mega Eco ran at about one third of capacity).
- Indicative Vietnam SME CAPEX for 5–10 imported Indian presses: about 5–10 × 30–100M VND for machines, plus dies, washing tanks, a dryer, UV equipment, a workshop fit-out and working capital to stock sheaths for the off-season. A plausible total is 0.5–2 billion VND. This is an inference with no Vietnamese source.

### Gaps
- KVIC and NCDC PDF details (labour count, wages, utility lines, depreciation) could not be fetched.

## 5. Vietnamese producers: process and scale

### Takeaway
Mega Eco Co., Ltd. (Nghĩa Hành, Quảng Ngãi; director Nguyễn Văn Tuyến; founded in 2019) is the best-documented Vietnamese producer. It uses Indian presses adapted locally, has 9–10 machines with 5 dies each, makes about 500,000–600,000 products a month, employs 15–25 workers, has revenue above 2 billion VND a year, and exports to the US, Canada, the Netherlands, Poland, Korea, Japan, Singapore and China. It also supplied Vietjet. Other brands (ArecaViet, IPAK, Green Joy) sell mo cau trays and boxes on the domestic market.

### Cited Findings
- The plant was founded in 2019 in Nghĩa Hành after the founder learned about Indian areca tableware, and it imported its machines from India. — [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/nguoi-dua-mo-cau-ra-the-gioi-4518258.html); [Báo Đầu tư](https://baodautu.vn/doanh-nhan-nguyen-van-tuyen-giam-doc-cong-ty-tnhh-mega-eco-dua-mo-cau-viet-nam-ra-the-gioi-d221385.html)
- Scale: 10 presses and about 500,000 products/month in one report, versus 9 presses × 5 dies and about 600,000/month in another. In 2021 it had 5 presses at 200 per machine per day. — [CafeF Apr 2024](https://cafef.vn/nguoi-dua-mo-cau-xuat-ngoai-188240407093726494.chn); [Công Thương](https://congthuong.vn/quang-ngai-bien-mo-cau-thanh-san-pham-xuat-khau-455559.html); [khoinghiep.net.vn](https://khoinghiep.net.vn/anh-nguyen-van-tuyen-khoi-nghiep-thanh-cong-voi-mo-cau-tuong-chung-bo-di-kiem-duoc-hang-tram-trieu-dong-a1425.html)
