## 1. Diện tích và sản lượng cau theo tỉnh; chu kỳ sốt giá / rớt giá cau tươi

### Takeaway
No national statistic for areca area could be found. GSO does not publish areca as a separate crop. Bến Tre's own Sở NN&PTNT said in 2022 that it could not count its areca area. The scattered local figures point to the South-Central coast and the Central Highlands: Quảng Ngãi (Nghĩa Hành, Sơn Tây) has ~2,000+ ha across a few districts, Quảng Nam ~926 ha and Đắk Lắk 1,400–2,000+ ha. Driven by Chinese demand, prices swung from a record ~85,000–100,000 đ/kg (2024–mid 2025) down to ~7,000–20,000 đ/kg (late 2025). That boom pushed a wave of new planting, which will raise sheath supply from about 2027–2029 regardless of nut prices.

### Inferences
- Palms planted in the 2024–2025 boom bear after ~4 years (Bến Tre's warning cites this), but they shed sheaths from about year 3–4. Sheath supply should therefore keep rising through 2027–2029, even if low nut prices discourage new planting.
- The big risk is that a long nut-price slump leads farmers to cut down or neglect plantings, especially "cau tròn" stands that China now refuses. No report of large-scale felling was found, but it is a plausible downside.
- A rough national total, built from fragments (Quảng Ngãi ~2,000+ ha in two districts, Quảng Nam ~926 ha, Đắk Lắk ~2,000 ha, plus scattered Mekong/Northern hedgerow palms), is probably several thousand to ~10,000+ ha of concentrated plantings. That is far smaller than India's ~950,000 ha. This is an inference, not an official figure.
- The Mekong Delta (old Bến Tre, Trà Vinh, Vĩnh Long, Sóc Trăng) has many palms, but they are dispersed as hedges and intercrops. Collection cost per sheath is likely higher there than in Quảng Ngãi's concentrated gardens.

### Gaps
- No GSO / Bộ NN&MT national areca area or production statistic for 2022–2026.
- No area figures found for Bình Định, Phú Yên, Thanh Hóa, Trà Vinh, Vĩnh Long or Sóc Trăng.
- No full-year 2024 or 2025 export tonnage. No direct reports of farmers cutting down areca after the 2025 crash.

## 2. Số mo/cây/năm, kích thước, tỷ lệ dùng được, mùa vụ, bảo quản

### Takeaway
A palm sheds roughly 5–10 sheaths a year, and about 4–5 per palm are usable after sorting (Indian data). Tiền Phong estimated ~12,500 sheaths/ha/year for Quảng Ngãi. The main Vietnamese shedding and collection season runs from tháng 3 to tháng 10. Sheaths from tháng 3–6 are the largest and best. Late-year (rainy season) sheaths are smaller, mould easily and sell for less. Farmers sun-dry sheaths for 3–4 days, flattened under weights. Factories accept only dry, untorn, mould-free sheaths and keep stock in moisture-proof rooms.

### Cited Findings
- About 10 sheaths/palm/year, with ~1,600 palms/ha (Bhargav & Gaikwad 2012, cited in an economic analysis). — [ResearchGate: Eco-friendly Arecanut Leaf Sheath Products: An Economic Analysis](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/337160284_Eco-friendly_Arecanut_Leaf_Sheath_Products_An_Economic_Analysis)
- Trade sources: 5–8 sheaths shed per year, of which ~4–5 are usable per palm after sorting. Others say 7–8. A Meghalaya agroforestry article says 5–6/year after 4 years of planting. Some manufacturer blogs claim 12–15, which looks inflated. — [Where Agroforestry meets Agriculture (Meghalaya)](https://whereagroforestrymeetsagriculture.com/rural-entrepreneurship-opportunities-from-arecanut-palm-in-meghalaya/); [Plant and Plates](https://plantandplates.com/lifecycle-of-palm-leaf-disposables/); [Foliware](https://foliware.com/consumer-education/)
- Vietnam: ~12,500 sheaths per ha per year, worth ~12.5 million đ/ha/year to the farmer at 1,000 đ/mo. — [Tiền Phong](https://tienphong.vn/bien-phe-pham-mo-cau-thanh-hang-xuat-khau-post1626654.tpo)
- Season: sheaths fall from tháng 3 to tháng 10. — [VOV5](https://vovworld.vn/vi-VN/viet-nam-quoc-gia-khoi-nghiep/san-xuat-bat-dia-tu-mo-cau-o-quang-ngai-2211110.vov5)
- Tháng 3–6 sheaths are the largest and best looking and mostly fetch the top price. Later in the year they are smaller, more prone to mould and cheaper. Sheaths wider than ~20 cm have high commercial value. — [Đại Đoàn Kết (Quảng Ngãi: Từ mo cau bỏ đi đến sản phẩm "xanh")](https://daidoanket.vn/quang-ngai-tu-mo-cau-bo-di-den-san-pham-xanh-mang-lai-thu-nhap.html); [Dân Việt – Trang trại Việt](https://trangtraiviet.danviet.vn/nhat-thu-bo-di-roi-phoi-kho-nong-dan-ban-cho-co-so-san-xuat-chen-dia-voi-gia-500-1000-dong-moi-cai-d1430459.html)
- Primary processing: collectors pick intact sheaths that are not rotten or damp, cut off the leaf blade and sun-dry them for 3–4 days, weighted down to stop curling and shrinking. Then traders come to buy. — [Dân Việt – Trang trại Việt](https://trangtraiviet.danviet.vn/nhat-thu-bo-di-roi-phoi-kho-nong-dan-ban-cho-co-so-san-xuat-chen-dia-voi-gia-500-1000-dong-moi-cai-d1430459.html); [Đại Đoàn Kết](https://daidoanket.vn/quang-ngai-tu-mo-cau-bo-di-den-san-pham-xanh-mang-lai-thu-nhap.html)
- Factories accept only dry, untorn, mould-free sheaths. — [Làng nghề Việt](https://langngheviet.com.vn/quang-ngai-san-pham-than-thien-moi-truong-tu-mo-cau-5465.html)
- A Huế producer stores sheaths in insulated, moisture-proof rooms because humid weather makes material mould. Factory process: wash, blow off dust, hot-press, then UV sterilise. — [Người Đô Thị via Báo Mới](https://baomoi.com/chang-trai-khoi-nghiep-voi-mo-cau-c55082577.epi); [Nông nghiệp & Môi trường](https://nongnghiepmoitruong.vn/mo-cau-tu-phe-pham-tro-thanh-huu-ich-d754446.html)

### Inferences
- For planning, use **4–6 usable sheaths/palm/year**, or roughly 8,000–12,500 per ha for dense gardens (Tiền Phong's figure is at the high end).
- A factory making 1 million products/year at ~1–2 products per sheath (no reliable conversion found) needs ~0.5–1 million good sheaths. At ~10,000 usable sheaths/ha, that means collecting from about 50–100+ ha of bearing gardens, more where collection rates are low.
- The best raw material comes in tháng 3–6, which matches the dry season in the South-Central coast. Central Vietnam's rainy season (≈ tháng 9–12) is the mould and quality risk window. The factory needs to buy and store a dry-season buffer stock, with mechanical drying or low-humidity storage.

### Gaps
- No Vietnamese agronomic study of sheath counts per palm, or of the usable (A-grade) rate in Vietnam.
- No reliable sheath-to-product conversion ratio, and no Vietnam-specific shelf-life data for dried sheaths.

## 3. Giá mo, mô hình thu gom, các nhà máy Việt Nam đang dùng mo cau

### Takeaway
The farm-gate price for dried sheaths in Quảng Ngãi is **500–700 đ/mo for small or low-grade sheaths and ~1,000 đ/mo for large, good-colour ones** (2024–2026 reports). Collection runs through elderly villagers and farmers who pick up fallen sheaths, local traders, ~20 collection points and a weekly "chợ mo cau" market in Nghĩa Hành. The anchor buyer is **Công ty TNHH Mega Eco** (CCN Đồng Dinh, Nghĩa Hành, Quảng Ngãi). It sources from ~1,000 local elderly collectors, exports ~90% of output (Mỹ, Hàn Quốc, Canada, Hà Lan) and plans 5 factories nationwide to cut raw-material transport costs.

### Cited Findings
- Price: small or poor-quality sheaths 500–700 đ/cái, large good-colour sheaths ~1,000 đ/cái. One collector gathers 200+ sheaths/week, and many people earn hundreds of thousands of đ per week. — [Dân Việt – Trang trại Việt](https://trangtraiviet.danviet.vn/nhat-thu-bo-di-roi-phoi-kho-nong-dan-ban-cho-co-so-san-xuat-chen-dia-voi-gia-500-1000-dong-moi-cai-d1430459.html); [Dân Trí 06/04/2026 "Nhặt mo cau bán kiếm hàng trăm nghìn đồng"](https://dantri.com.vn/lao-dong-viec-lam/nhat-mo-cau-ban-kiem-hang-tram-nghin-dong-20260406120201680.htm)

About 20 buying points provide extra income for hundreds of households. — [Đại Đoàn Kết](https://daidoanket.vn/quang-ngai-tu-mo-cau-bo-di-den-san-pham-xanh-mang-lai-thu-nhap.html); [Người Quan Sát (weekly sheath market)](https://nguoiquansat.vn/nong-dan-quang-ngai-lap-cho-quai-la-moi-tuan-hop-mot-lan-khong-ban-ca-thit-ma-ban-rac-223354.html); [Công Thương: "Quảng Ngãi: Độc đáo chợ mo cau"](https://congthuong.vn/quang-ngai-doc-dao-cho-mo-cau-457044.html)

### Inferences
- Raw material cost is ~500–1,000 đ per sheath, about 0.02–0.04 USD. If 1 sheath gives 1–2 products selling at 2,000–3,000 đ ex-works, raw material is roughly 20–50% of the ex-works price. That share is significant, so grading and the reject rate drive margins.
- Nghĩa Hành – Tư Nghĩa – Sơn Tây (Quảng Ngãi) is the proven sourcing cluster, but the incumbent Mega Eco is already there. A new factory would compete for the same collectors. The alternatives are another cluster (Quảng Nam/Đà Nẵng mới, Đắk Lắk, Kon Tum cũ) or a pre-agreed share with existing traders.
- The collection model rests on elderly people's spare labour and sheaths that would otherwise be burned. Supply is elastic only up to the price at which picking them up is worth the effort. A price increase (e.g. to 1,200–1,500 đ) could raise the collection rate.

### Gaps
- No verified annual capacity or sheath-consumption figures for Mega Eco or Phúc Thịnh.
- No data on Mekong Delta sheath collection or on factories in the South. No sign of cooperatives (HTX) formally organising sheath collection.

## 4. So sánh với Ấn Độ (Karnataka, Kerala, Assam) và Sri Lanka

### Takeaway
India is far larger in scale: ~9.5–9.6 lakh ha and ~13.7–14 lakh t of nuts (2023–24). Karnataka alone has ~6.8–6.9 lakh ha (>70% of output). An academic estimate puts India's available sheaths at ~1 billion a year (~233,000 t). Sri Lanka has only ~12,000–13,000 ha, grown as an intercrop, and its areca economy is nut exports. Vietnam's concentrated areca area is roughly two orders of magnitude smaller than India's. Vietnam can compete on proximity to East Asian buyers, not on raw-material volume.

### Cited Findings
- India 2023–24: ~14 lakh t from 9.49 lakh ha (Forumias). A Lok Sabha answer gives 9.638 lakh ha and 13.739 lakh t. Karnataka has 6.76–6.90 lakh ha and ~10 lakh t, more than 70% of output. — [Forumias](https://forumias.com/blog/?p=358740); [Lok Sabha AU1340](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/2984151/1/AU1340_8ihYr3.pdf); [DASD](https://www.dasd.gov.in/adminimage/Arecanut_area_and_production.pdf)
- Kerala ~94,100 ha (CEIC "2024") with ~104,000 t. Assam production ~47,400 t (2024). — [CEIC area](https://www.ceicdata.com/en/india/area-of-horticulture-crops-in-major-states-plantations-arecanut); [CEIC production](https://www.ceicdata.com/en/india/production-of-horticulture-crops-in-major-states-plantations-arecanut)

Older data gives ~11,968 ha. Third-party trade data puts raw areca nut exports at ~60.6 million USD in 2023 (unverified). Leaf plates are made by artisans in the wet zone (Kegalle, Kandy), with no volume data. — [Daily News LK 19/03/2024](https://www.dailynews.lk/2024/03/19/admin-catagories/breaking-news/455197/decision-to-extend-betel-nut-cultivation-as-an-export-crop/); [Tridge](https://www.tridge.com/tridge-woods/raw-areca-nut/LK); [Lakpura](https://lakpura.com/cs/pages/areca-catechu)

### Inferences
- Indian Karnataka clusters such as Shivamogga and Dakshina Kannada can support hundreds of plate units. Vietnam can support only a handful of medium factories unless supply is aggregated across provinces. The finding that Assam's rainy-season shortages and poor intercrop leaf quality limit production is a useful parallel for Vietnam's scattered Mekong hedgerow palms.

### Gaps
- No India farm-gate sheath price (₹/sheath) found in this search, and no Sri Lanka sheath-plate industry size.

## 5. Rủi ro chuỗi cung ứng

### Takeaway
The main risks are: (1) **rainy-season mould and smaller late-year sheaths** (tháng 9–12 in Central Vietnam); (2) **dependence on a nut-price cycle controlled by Chinese buyers**, which could lead to plantation neglect or felling; (3) **competition for collectors** with the incumbent Mega Eco in Quảng Ngãi; (4) **dispersed supply and transport costs** (Mega Eco itself plans 5 regional factories to cut them); and (5) unquantified **pesticide residue and food-safety** questions on sheaths from intensively sprayed areca gardens.

### Cited Findings
- Late-year sheaths are smaller, mould easily and sell for less. Humid weather in Huế forces moisture-proof storage. — [Đại Đoàn Kết](https://daidoanket.vn/quang-ngai-tu-mo-cau-bo-di-den-san-pham-xanh-mang-lai-thu-nhap.html); [Báo Mới / Người Đô Thị](https://baomoi.com/chang-trai-khoi-nghiep-voi-mo-cau-c55082577.epi)
- India: moisture in sheaths damages plates, there are rainy-season shortages, and supply is vulnerable to floods and droughts in concentrated growing areas. — [NCDC DPR](https://ncdc.in/documents/downloads/12150805206.Sample-DPR_Manufacturing-of-Palm-Leaf-Plate.pdf); [Value Market Research (market-report marketing page, low reliability)](https://www.valuemarketresearch.com/report/areca-plates-market)
- A dryer vendor argues for closed-loop drying and moisture control to prevent mould and aflatoxin. This is a commercial source, so its claims are unverified. — [Deair](https://deair.com.vn/san-xuat-dia-mo-cau-ba-mia-quy-trinh-say-chuan-fda)
- Price volatility: up-fast/down-fast cycles repeated within 12–18 months, and China stopped buying "cau tròn" in 2025. — [PLO](https://plo.vn/thuong-lai-trung-quoc-quay-xe-nong-dan-quang-ngai-dieu-dung-vi-gia-cau-rot-tham-post876286.html); [Đại biểu Nhân dân](https://daibieunhandan.vn/dak-lak-gia-cau-tuoi-giam-manh-khi-thi-truong-buoc-vao-cuoi-vu-10401746.html)
- Mega Eco's plan for 5 factories nationwide to cut raw-material transport costs shows that logistics is a real cost driver. — [megaeco.vn](https://megaeco.vn/pages/about-us)
- Before the industry existed, sheaths were burned or discarded. Their main competing use is as fuel or waste. — [Dân Việt – Trang trại Việt](https://trangtraiviet.danviet.vn/nhat-thu-bo-di-roi-phoi-kho-nong-dan-ban-cho-co-so-san-xuat-chen-dia-voi-gia-500-1000-dong-moi-cai-d1430459.html)

### Inferences
- The competition is not alternative uses (firewood, traditional fans or "quạt mo") but other mo cau factories. As more factories open, the 500–1,000 đ price is likely to rise.
- Mitigations: buy and store a large tháng 3–6 stock; invest in mechanical dryers and low-humidity storage; publish a grading spec (≥20 cm wide, no tears or mould); run multi-province collection hubs (Quảng Ngãi, Quảng Nam/Đà Nẵng, Đắk Lắk/Kon Tum cũ); and test sheaths for pesticide residues and mycotoxins, as needed for exports to the EU, US and Korea.
- Sheaths are collected after natural shedding, so supply does not depend on harvesting nuts. But it does depend on palms staying in the ground, so a prolonged nut-price collapse is the main structural risk.

### Gaps
- No data on pesticide or herbicide use in Vietnamese areca gardens, and no residue tests on Vietnamese sheaths.
- No quantified mould loss rate, and no data on logistics cost per sheath or per km.
- No evidence of competing industrial uses (e.g. biomass pellets) in Vietnam.
